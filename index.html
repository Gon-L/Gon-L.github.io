Viral Clips

A personal tool by Gon for making and publishing short videos.

Viral Clips finds trending topics each day, makes short original vertical videos about the ones its owner picks, and sends the approved videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews and publishes every video in the TikTok app.

It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.
