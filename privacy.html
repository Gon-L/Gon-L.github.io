Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Viral Clips ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Gon for their own TikTok account. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What the app accesses

  • When the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok gives the app an access token with the permissions the owner approves (basic profile info and video upload).
  • The app uses that token only to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts.

What the app stores

  • The TikTok token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository. It is not stored anywhere else.
  • The app does not collect, store or process data about any other TikTok users or viewers.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share any data with third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok, at the owner's request.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions) and delete the stored token from GitHub.
